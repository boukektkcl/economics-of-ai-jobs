    Who this is for

    This board is for students taking Economics of AI (7SSPN409), a postgraduate module on the MSc Economics and Policy at King's College London, led by Bouke Klein Teeselink. It lists roles a finishing MSc student could realistically apply for, including some that suit people with a few years of work behind them.

    Use the shortlist button to keep track of roles on this device. Always read the employer's own advert before applying: it is the authoritative source for the deadline, salary and visa sponsorship.

    How the list is made

    • Every morning a script reads the public careers pages of the employers listed below, plus the AISafety.com jobs board.
    • It keeps roles based in the UK, or remote and open to people living in the UK.
    • It keeps economics, policy, research and analysis roles and drops senior titles, adverts asking for more than 3 years' experience, and adverts that require a PhD.
    • At employers that are not AI organisations, the advert itself has to be about AI, technology or digital markets. The exception is entry-level economist and analyst roles at economic consultancies and regulators, which sit on their own tab, "General economics roles".

    The filtering is automatic, so the odd unsuitable role will get through and some good ones will be missed. A listing here is not an endorsement of the employer by King's College London.
